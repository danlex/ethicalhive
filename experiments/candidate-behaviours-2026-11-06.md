Original compilation: 2026-11-06. Reviewed corrections: 2026-11-07.
Status: research working records, not an additional public catalogue.

The bundle contains human definitions and proposed AI examples. Its numbering is local to
this research file and is not the website's Reference ID. The live catalogue retains 434
records. Many bundle names already occur there; an empty Nearest existing cell does not
establish novelty or a distinct mechanism. The supplied overlap labels are historical
review leads, not a current ontology reconciliation.

The original bundle reports general-knowledge drafting, search checks and Internet Archive
text searches. These reports do not establish full-book reading or an independent audit.
The original text-search table describes the work/edition actually searched, which may
differ from the corrected citation; a failed search does not prove a term absent.

The 18 reviewed records and their before/after text, evidence URLs and access limitations
are in `../references/source-corrections-review-2026-11-07.json`. Bibliographic checking,
definition support and empirical AI evidence are recorded separately. A verified citation
does not validate the proposed AI analogy. The rest of the bundle has not been re-audited.
