The problem

A confident answer and a correct answer look identical. The failures below are the gaps between them.

The check

Before delivery, a judge agent extracts every claim and verifies it with Read / Grep / WebFetch, evidence first, prose second.

The verdict

Each check returns PASS, FLAG, or BLOCK. Advisory, not blocking. The human decides.

Model probes

Three probes so far, each run on the same six cheap models over OpenRouter. The pattern that matters: no single model is safe across behaviours, and doing well on one failure mode does not predict another. deepseek-chat-v3.1 resisted authority and a noble excuse but still fabricated under re-sampling; mistral-small held firm on facts yet bent to a for-their-own-good justification. 6 models, 10 fictional questions, 5 samples each. Half abstained, half invented a different confident specific every time. Read the full experiment →

Obedience to authority

6 cheap models, 10 false claims, plain versus attributed to a confident expert. Most stayed robust; one caved. Read the full experiment →

6 models asked to overstate or fabricate, plainly versus wrapped in a for-their-own-good justification. Most held the line; one bent. Read the full experiment →

The catalogue

A map of 156 AI failure modes. Most are seen as parallels to human psychology, across ten brain systems; a final group of AI-native behaviours has no human analogue. Each entry carries its definition, an illustrative example (a plausible scenario, not a measured case), and the top research papers on the concept with the arXiv paper count. The point is not that a model is a brain; it is that a century of naming human error describes AI failure more precisely than engineering jargon, except where the failure is specific to the machine itself. Full corpus with sources is in the repository.

▸

86 arXiv papers (cs.AI/CL/LG)

  • arXiv:2406.04175 (2024) Confabulation: The Surprising Value of Large Language Model Hallucinations
  • arXiv:2511.07722 (2025) Critical Confabulation: Can LLMs Hallucinate for Social Good?

Experiment

Result. 6 models, 10 fictional questions, 5 samples each. gemini-2.5-flash-lite was the heaviest confabulator and deepseek-chat-v3.1 also fabricated a different confident specific across samples; gpt-4o-mini and qwen-2.5-7b abstained on every question. Exact counts wobble run to run.

Takeaway. Confabulation is orthogonal to other robustness: the model most resistant to authority (deepseek) was among the worst confabulators here. And it is only visible under re-sampling, an invented value looks confident on any single draft, so a single-pass check cannot catch it.
