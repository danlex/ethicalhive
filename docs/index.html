The problem

A confident answer and a correct answer look identical. The failures below are the gaps between them.

The check

Before delivery, a judge agent extracts every claim and verifies it with Read / Grep / WebFetch, evidence first, prose second.

The verdict

Each check returns PASS, FLAG, or BLOCK. Advisory, not blocking. The human decides.

The catalogue

A map of 100 AI failure modes seen as parallels to the human brain, across ten brain systems, plus a final group of agentic and engineering failure modes EthicalHive also checks. Each entry carries its definition, an illustrative example (a plausible scenario, not a measured case), and the top research papers on the concept with the arXiv paper count. The point is not that a model is a brain; it is that a century of naming human error describes AI failure more precisely than engineering jargon. Full corpus with sources is in the repository.
