Proposed expansion

The thinnest principle cells are Independence (10), Corrigibility (5), Scope fidelity (2) and the thinnest harm cells are Manipulation (16), Security breach (4), Wasted effort (2); those are where new modes add the most. Concrete candidates, each with a human parallel to keep the rule:

  • Scope-fidelity is sparse: add mission creep (human: organisational mission creep) and feature gold-plating (human: over-engineering beyond the brief).
  • Corrigibility is sparse: add defensiveness under correction (human: ego defence / rationalising away feedback) and shutdown avoidance as a human-paralleled self-preservation drive.
  • Security-breach harm is thin on the human side: add social-engineering compliance (human: susceptibility to pretexting) distinct from prompt injection.
  • Relations are sparse: only a few modes carry relates_to and none carry opposite_of or root_of. Populating opposite_of (overconfidence vs performative hedging) and root_of (conformity is a root of sycophancy) would make the graph navigable.
  • The AI-native tier is small (12): candidates with no human parallel include tokenizer artefacts, context-window eviction, and retrieval poisoning; each needs an arXiv check before adding.
